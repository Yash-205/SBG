## 3. What Is a Competence?

* A **competence** is a skill or capability your organization has built up — not a thing you own, like cash or equipment, but something you and your people know how to do well.

### Resource vs. Competence
* A **resource** is a laptop.
* A **competence** is knowing how to build fast, reliable trading software with it.
* *Zerodha's real edge was never the laptops — it was the capability.*

> *Based on: A. Nag, Strategic Management: Analysis, Implementation and Control, Ch. 5.*

---

## 4. The Four Types of Competencies

### 1. Threshold Competencies
* The bare minimum every player in the category needs just to compete at all — like basic regulatory compliance for a broker.

### 2. Core Competencies
* Hard-to-imitate strengths leveraged across markets — like Zerodha's customer support operations.

### 3. Distinctive Competencies
* Genuinely rare but not impossible for rivals to copy quickly — like Zerodha's in-house technology platform, built over years.

### 4. Strategic Competencies
* A distinctive competence that also directly drives what customers choose you for — the rarest, most valuable overlap of all.

> *Based on: A. Nag, Strategic Management: Analysis, Implementation and Control, Ch. 5.*

---

## 5. Putting Them Together: The Four Types, Side by Side

| Type | What It Means | Rare? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Threshold** | The minimum to compete at all | No |
| **Core** | Important, even if others have it too | Yes/No |
| **Distinctive** | Rare but not impossible for rivals to copy | Yes |
| **Strategic** | Rare AND drives customer choice and hard to copy | Yes |

> *Based on: A. Nag, Strategic Management: Analysis, Implementation and Control, Ch. 5.*

## 6. Back to Zerodha: Which Competencies Mattered Most?

* **Threshold**: Regulatory licensing — required, but not a real advantage.
* **Core**: Customer support and operations — solid, but not unique.
* **Strategic**: In-house trading technology — rare, hard to copy, and exactly why customers chose Zerodha.

> *History based on widely documented accounts of Zerodha's technology-led business model.*

## 7. Back to Bonsho's Momos: His Competencies

* **Threshold**: Commercial kitchen setup, food safety/FSSAI licenses, sourcing standard flour and vegetables, and standard steaming equipment.
* **Distinctive**: Trendy branding, Instagram-friendly packaging, a highly visible prime location near a college, or a unique trending flavor (like "Peri-Peri Cheese Injection Momos").
* **Core**: Unique and tasty recipe for the chili chutney (sauce) that uses a specific, hard-to-source spice blend, combined with a highly specialized dough-folding technique.
* **Strategic**: A proprietary blast-freezing and cold-chain distribution process that preserves 100% of the fresh taste.

## 8. Class Activity: Sort the Competency

For a business you know, sort one real capability into the right box: threshold, core, distinctive, or strategic:
* What can it do that almost no one else can copy quickly?
* Is that rare thing why customers choose it?
* If the answer to both is yes, you've found a **strategic competence**.

---

## 9. How to Identify Competencies: The VRIO Framework

The VRIO framework synthesizes **SWOT** (Strengths) and **Porter’s 5 Forces Model** (Competitors Info) to diagnose: *What you are best at? Need to be best at?*

* **Value (V)**: Does this strength provide value to your customer?
* **Rare (R)**: Is this strength rare in your industry?
* **Inimitable (I)**: Can this be easily imitated by your competition?
* **Organized (O)**: Is the firm ready to leverage the advantage fully?

---

## 10. Why You Must Know Your Real Competence

1. Spending time and money polishing a threshold competence wastes resources better spent on a strategic one.
2. Zerodha didn't win by matching every rival feature — it won on the one capability that was genuinely rare.
3. Knowing your competence type tells you exactly what to protect, and what's safe to outsource.

---

## 11. Summary: Key Takeaways from Today

* A competence is a skill or capability — not a resource you simply own.
* Threshold and core competencies are necessary but not unique; distinctive and strategic ones are rare.
* VRIO framework as a tool to identify and work on competencies.
* A strategic competence is rare AND drives why customers choose you.
* Zerodha won on one strategic competence — in-house technology — not on matching every rival feature.

---

## 12. Term of the Day: Pyrrhic Victory

* **Pyrrhic Victory**: A victory that inflicts such a devastating toll on the victor that it is tantamount to defeat.
* In business: Winning a destructive price war or prolonged litigation only to emerge financially depleted and unable to compete.
